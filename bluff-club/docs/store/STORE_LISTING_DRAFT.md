**Full description:**

Everyone knows. Someone's pretending.

Bluff Club is a pass-the-phone party game for 3 to 10 players in the same room. Each
player privately peeks at their card. Everyone sees the same secret word — except one
player, the bluffer, who gets nothing and has to fake it.

Take turns giving one short clue, argue it out, then vote in secret. Catch the bluffer
and everyone else scores. Accuse the wrong person, or deadlock twice, and the bluffer
takes the points.

• One phone, no accounts, no sign-up
• The free General pack has 100 everyday words and unlimited rounds
• Private hold-to-reveal cards and secret voting on the same phone
• Optional discussion timer: 60, 90 or 120 seconds, or off
• Ties get a short extra discussion and one runoff vote
• Session scores across rounds
• Free gameplay works without an internet connection

Optional once-off packs, each 60 words: Food Fight, Office Chaos, SA Game Night and
Wild Card. The Party Bundle includes exactly these four packs. No subscriptions and no
ads. Buying or restoring packs needs an internet connection.
